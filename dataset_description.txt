WiDS Datathon 2020. One row is one stay in an intensive care unit.
The outcome is hospital death: the patient died during this hospital stay.
The columns are clinical measurements, mostly from the first 24 hours.
Column names are lowercase words joined by underscores.
d1_ means the first 24 hours, h1_ means the first hour.
_min is the lowest value, _max is the highest.
_apache means the reading used in the APACHE severity score.
The measurements include age, heart rate, blood pressure, Glasgow coma scale,
oxygen saturation, and length of stay before the intensive care unit.
The task is to explain which of these measurements pushed one patient's risk of death.
